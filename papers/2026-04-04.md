<div class="abstract-content"><div class="abstract-en"><div class="abstract-lang">Abstract</div>Expressive generative models have advanced robotic manipulation by capturing complex, multi-modal action distributions over temporally extended trajectories. <div class="abstract-content"><div class="abstract-en"><div class="abstract-lang">Abstract</div>Estimating physical properties is critical for safe and efficient autonomous robotic manipulation, particularly during contact-rich interactions. In such settings, vision and tactile sensing provide complementary information about object geometry, pose, inertia, stiffness, and contact dynamics, such as stick-slip behavior.
